Fall & Impact Detection
Opt-in Motion Heuristic

With your saved location and motion consent, monitoring starts after one explicit enable action. Small jolts are ignored: a possible fall needs a near-free-fall motion followed by a strong impact (over 35 m/s²) within 1.2 seconds, then gives you 15 seconds to cancel. This is a conservative heuristic, not medical or guaranteed fall detection.
